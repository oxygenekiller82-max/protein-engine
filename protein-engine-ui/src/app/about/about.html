About Protein Engine

Protein Engine is a bioinformatics platform designed to help researchers generate, analyze, and compare protein sequences efficiently.

Our Mission

To accelerate protein research by providing intelligent tools for sequence generation and comparison based on key biochemical properties.

Key Features

Generation

Generate protein sequences based on hydrophobicity, mass, stability and binding parameters.

Comparison

Compare two sequences side by side with visual charts for each biochemical property.

Logging

Track all your generation and comparison history in one place.

Storage

Save and retrieve sequences for future analysis sessions.

Technology Stack

Built with Angular, powered by modern bioinformatics algorithms.
